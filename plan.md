### Le mythe de la productivité et de la technique

Studies :

### 1. Dimension: Delivery Cycle and Shipping
* **MIT Sloan & University of Pennsylvania Study (2026)**
    * **Type:** Academic
    * **Main Impact:** Raw coding activity surges by +180%, but the final deployment rate only increases by 30% due to bottlenecks during human code reviews and testing phases.
* **Harness SEI Study / Enterprise Case Studies (2023-2025)**
    * **Type:** Private
    * **Main Impact:** A recorded ~10% increase in the volume of Pull Requests (PRs) submitted and a cycle time reduction of approximately 3.5 hours.

### 2. Dimension: Speed and Individual Coding Productivity
* **Stanford University Git Study (2025)**
    * **Type:** Academic
    * **Main Impact:** A net overall productivity gain between 15% and 20%. The impact drops drastically on complex legacy architectures (*brownfield*) compared to new projects (*greenfield*).
* **GitHub & Microsoft Research Study (Peng et al., 2022-2023)**
    * **Type:** Private (with rigorous scientific methodology)
    * **Main Impact:** Developers equipped with AI completed a standardized programming task 55.8% faster than the control group.

### 3. Dimension: Learning and Skill Retention
* **Anthropic Study - "How AI Impacts Skill Formation" (January 2026)**
    * **Type:** Private (AI Research Lab)
    * **Main Impact:** Developers using AI scored 17% lower on a follow-up quiz conducted without AI. Passive tool usage acts as a "temporary skill rental" and hinders long-term knowledge retention.

### 4. Dimension: Code Maintainability and Technical Debt
* **Empirical Trial on "Downstream" Maintenance (2026)**
    * **Type:** Academic (Pre-registered study)
    * **Main Impact:** AI acts as an amplifier: it accelerates good architectural practices for senior developers but amplifies poor architecture (generating "slop code") for junior profiles.
* **GitClear Report / Repository Analysis (2024-2025)**
    * **Type:** Private
    * **Main Impact:** A significant increase in redundant code and a decline in code reuse rate, raising alarms about long-term technical debt accumulation.

### 5. Dimension: Developer Experience (DevEx) and Satisfaction
* **DORA Research Report & DX SPACE Framework (2024-2025)**
    * **Type:** Mixed (Academic and Industrial Research Consortium)
    * **Main Impact:** AI markedly improves perceived job satisfaction by taking over repetitive tasks (tests, boilerplate), freeing up mental space for complex problem-solving.
